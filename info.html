Roblox Scanner Script

RUNS IN YOUR EXECUTOR · SCANS THE WHOLE SERVER
Runs in your executor
Paste the script into your Roblox executor and run it. Nothing else to install.
Scans the whole server
It goes through every player in the server you're in and checks each one's public Roblox profile against our records.
Shows linked accounts
Usernames and IDs get matched to connected accounts and any exposure we have, so you see the whole lobby, not one player at a time.
It only reads public profile data of players already in your server. Follow Roblox's rules and your local laws when you use it.
